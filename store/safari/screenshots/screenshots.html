/u/1/ breaks. Your email doesn't.

Google numbers your accounts by sign-in order. Deeper Link swaps the number for the account's email.

Gmail
mail.google.com/mail/u/1/#inbox/FMfcgzQX… = 2nd account signed in
mail.google.com/mail/u/?authuser=alice@example.com#inbox/FMfcgzQX… Always Alice's inbox
Drive
drive.google.com/drive/u/2/folders/1AbCdEf… = 3rd account signed in
drive.google.com/drive/folders/1AbCdEf…?authuser=alice@company.com Always the work account
Docs
docs.google.com/document/d/1DocId…/edit = your default account
docs.google.com/document/d/1DocId…/edit?authuser=alice@company.com Always the work account

One click. Copied.
